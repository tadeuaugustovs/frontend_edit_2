# PM1.1 - Mission Statement

## Vision
To simplify and modernize the lifecycle management of Public Notices ("Editais"), empowering administrators with tools that are as beautiful as they are functional.

## Mission
Develop a responsive, secure, and intuitive frontend application that reduces administrative burden and increases transparency in public processes.

## Values
*   **User-Centricity**: Design for the end-user first.
*   **Excellence**: Pixel-perfect UI and robust code.
*   **Transparency**: Clear audit trails and process visibility.
